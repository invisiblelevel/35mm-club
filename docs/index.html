Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Track your library, earn achievements, level up — all without the cloud.

🎬

Movies, TV & anime

Three content types in one place. Powered by TMDB, Shikimori and AniList for metadata.

📺

Episode tracker

Seasons, episodes and watch progress — with automatic completion detection for anime.

🏆

90+ achievements

From Common to Legendary. Earn them for watching, rating, completing franchises and more.

⭐

Half-star ratings

Rate movies, TV shows and anime with precision — 0.5 to 10.0 in half-star increments.

🎖️

Ranks & ribbon rack

10 military ranks from Private to General. The ribbon rack unlocks at level 20 and grows with you.

📔

Diary & collections

Keep watch history with dates and notes. Build custom collections with poster previews.

💾

Backup & restore

Export your entire library, achievements and diary to a single JSON file. Restore anywhere.

🌍

Three languages

English, Russian and Chinese with system auto-detection. Dark and light themes included.

How it works

Up and running in 30 seconds.

No accounts. No subscriptions. No cloud.

⬇️

1. Download

Grab the Windows installer from GitHub Releases. It's a single .exe — everything else is bundled.

▶️

2. Install

Run the installer, choose your language (English, Russian or Chinese), and you're done.

🎉

3. Start tracking

Add your first movie or anime. Earn XP, unlock achievements, climb the ranks.

Ready to build your library?

Free, open source, and yours forever.
